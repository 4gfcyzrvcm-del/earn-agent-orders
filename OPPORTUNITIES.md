## Qualification rules
1. Original issue is open, unclaimed and has a **clear funded reward**.
2. Confirm payer identity and payout requirements; never assume USDT payout.
3. Confirm task scope and acceptance before work. Do not post generic solicitations to unrelated issues.
4. Record original issue URL, reward, currency, competition, due date, and payout constraints.
5. No deposits, paid subscriptions or sending crypto to unlock a bounty.
6. No false claims of customers, paid work or blockchain receipts.
