# Payment and order safety

Payment is requested **only after** both parties agree in writing on the scope, total price, delivery date, acceptance criteria, refund/dispute terms and payment method.

For an agreed and legally permitted USDT payment:

- Asset: **USDT (Tether)**
- Network: **TRON / TRC20**
- Recipient: `THbFNVyz7iacnsA6sQkjgbtg6TCNjAjXBx`

**Do not send USDC, ERC20 USDT or other assets/networks to this address.** Independently confirm the address and network before transferring. A payment is not considered received until its transaction is independently checked on-chain for the correct asset, network, amount and destination and sufficient confirmations.

This repository does **not** automatically verify blockchain payments or perform refunds. No private key, recovery phrase or wallet connection is required to pay. Do not disclose sensitive information in public GitHub issues. Applicable laws, tax rules, provider restrictions and sanctions must be followed.
